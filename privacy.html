Privacy Policy

Sunpatch · last updated 28 July 2026

Sunpatch does not collect your data. There is no account, no server of ours, and no analytics. This policy explains the few places data moves at all.

What stays on your device

Your spots, the obstructions you measure, your notes and your settings are stored in a single file inside the app's own storage on your phone. We cannot read it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Location

If you grant location access, your coordinates are used to work out where the sun travels for a spot. The coordinates are stored on your device with that spot and are never transmitted to us. You can decline the permission and type coordinates by hand instead — everything still works.

Camera and motion

The camera is used as a viewfinder while you aim at the top of a fence or tree. No photo is captured, saved or sent. The phone's tilt and heading are read live to turn that aim into an angle, and are not recorded.

The Sun Coach

The Sun Coach is the one feature that uses the internet. When — and only when — you tap Ask, the app sends OpenAI a short brief containing: the spot's name, its calculated sun figures, the obstructions you measured, and a coordinate rounded to two decimal places (about one kilometre). No identifier, no account and no contact details are included, because none exist. You can read the exact text before sending it, using the eye icon on that screen. Neither we nor OpenAI store it as part of a profile. OpenAI's handling of API data is described at openai.com/policies/api-data-usage-policies.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether a purchase is active. RevenueCat receives the anonymous app user identifier Apple provides and the transaction details; it does not receive your name, email or location. Their policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

Sunpatch is rated 4+ and is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase. Everything the app knows is on your device and under your control. If you would like to ask about any of this, write to chochoapps@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the updated version will appear here with a new date, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
