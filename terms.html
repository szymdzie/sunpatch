What the numbers are, and are not

Sunpatch calculates the position of the sun using a published astronomical algorithm and combines it with obstructions you measure yourself. The sun's position is accurate. Your measurements are estimates, and so the result is an estimate: a fence entered two metres too tall, or a bearing taken next to a steel railing, will move the answer.

Plant requirements are drawn from published horticultural guidance and are general, not specific to your soil, climate, water or shelter. Sunpatch tells you about light. It cannot tell you whether something will grow.

Do not rely on Sunpatch for decisions where being wrong is expensive or dangerous — sizing a solar installation, planning construction, or anything involving trees that are not yours. Permission and local rules come before any figure this app gives you.

The Sun Coach

The Sun Coach is part of Pro and needs an account, because it runs on a server and costs money for each answer. It is limited to a fair number of questions per hour.

Answers are written by a language model from the measurements the app computed. They are suggestions, not professional advice, and can be wrong. The sun figures the app calculates are not produced by the model.
